## 🎨 Color System

### Primary Color

- **Value**: `oklch(0.55 0.23 280)` - Deep Purple/Indigo
- **Usage**: Main buttons, links, important elements
- **Feel**: Professional, trustworthy, modern

### Accent Color

- **Value**: `oklch(0.65 0.2 260)` - Vibrant Cyan/Blue
- **Usage**: Secondary highlights, gradients
- **Feel**: Modern, energetic, complementary

### Secondary Color

- **Value**: `oklch(0.92 0.03 45)` - Golden Orange
- **Usage**: Badges, special highlights
- **Feel**: Warm, inviting, premium

### Background & Foreground

- **Light Mode**: Clean white backgrounds with dark text
- **Dark Mode**: Deep navy backgrounds with light text
- **Gradient Backgrounds**: Subtle transitions throughout
